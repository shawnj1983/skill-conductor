# Contributing

Keep the pair together. A PR that changes the skill must check the rule, and the other way around.

- `SKILL.md` stays under ~200 lines. Put depth in `reference.md` or `examples.md`.
- The rule stays under ~40 lines. Standing order only.
- Descriptions: third person, what + when.
- No secrets. No legal names. Public author is the LICENSE copyright holder.
- Do not add a second router skill.
